Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Feedlyx ("we", "the app") is a personal automation tool operated by the Feedlyx team. It publishes short videos about new game and movie releases to the operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Who uses the app

Feedlyx is not a public service. It is used only by its operator to post to the operator's own accounts. No other users can sign up.

2. Data we access from TikTok

When the operator connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • basic account information: open ID, display name and avatar;
  • access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos to that account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

The app does not access followers, messages, comments, viewing history or any data of other TikTok users.

3. How we use the data

The tokens are used only to upload videos created by Feedlyx to the connected account and to check the upload status. Account information is used only to show which account is connected.

4. Content we publish

Videos are generated automatically from publicly available information about game and movie releases: titles, descriptions, official trailers and store images. They contain no personal data of any person.

5. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted on a private server controlled by the operator and are never logged or shown in plain text. Access to the server is restricted to the operator.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok data with third parties. The data is not used for advertising or profiling.

7. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected. To stop all access, revoke Feedlyx in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. Or email fedotov.dev@gmail.com, and we will delete all stored data within 30 days.

8. Children

Feedlyx is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process data of children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published at this address with the date of the last update.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: fedotov.dev@gmail.com.
