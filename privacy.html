Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Feedlyx ("we", "the app") is a personal automation tool operated by the Feedlyx team. It publishes short videos about new game and movie releases to the operator's own social media accounts, including YouTube and TikTok. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Who uses the app

Feedlyx is not a public service. It is used only by its operator to post to the operator's own accounts. No other users can sign up.

2. Data we access from TikTok

When the operator connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • basic account information: open ID, display name and avatar;
  • access and refresh tokens that let the app upload videos to that account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

The app does not access followers, messages, comments, viewing history or any data of other TikTok users.

3. Data we access from Google (YouTube)

Feedlyx uses YouTube API Services. When the operator authorizes a YouTube channel with Google, the app requests two permissions and receives:

  • youtube.upload: the right to upload videos created by Feedlyx to that channel, with their title, description, tags and privacy status;
  • youtube.readonly: the channel name and ID, used only to show which channel is connected;
  • an OAuth refresh token that lets the app make these requests without signing in again.

The app does not read or change other videos, comments, subscriptions, playlists, watch history or any data of other YouTube users, and it does not delete anything from the channel.

Feedlyx's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the upload feature described here; it is not transferred to others, used for advertising, sold, or read by humans except when needed for security or to comply with the law.

By connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access from VK

When the operator connects VK through VK ID, the app receives the VK user ID and access and refresh tokens with the video, wall and groups permissions. They are used only to upload Feedlyx videos to, and post them on, the VK communities the operator manages. The app does not read messages, friends or other users' data. Access can be revoked at any time in VK ID settings (Connected services).

4. How we use the data

Tokens are used only to upload videos created by Feedlyx to the connected accounts and to check the upload status. Account and channel information is used only to show which account is connected.

5. Content we publish

Videos are generated automatically from publicly available information about game and movie releases: titles, descriptions, official trailers and store images. They contain no personal data of any person.

6. Storage and security

Tokens are stored on a private server controlled by the operator, in files and secrets readable only by the app, and are never logged or shown in plain text. Access to the server is restricted to the operator.

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share TikTok or Google data with third parties. The data is not used for advertising or profiling.

8. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while an account stays connected. To stop all access at any time:

  • YouTube: remove Feedlyx at Google Account → Third-party apps and services;
  • TikTok: revoke Feedlyx in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and we delete them. You can also email fedotov.dev@gmail.com, and we will delete all stored data within 30 days.

9. Children

Feedlyx is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process data of children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always published at this address with the date of the last update.

11. Contact
