New games and movies, in short videos

Every day Feedlyx picks the most popular new releases, cuts the best moments of their trailers into a vertical video with a voice-over, and posts it to our own channels: Telegram, YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

How it works

  1. Collect. New releases come from public sources: Steam for games, TMDB for movies.
  2. Make. The server picks trailer highlights, adds titles, prices and a short voice-over, and renders a 1080×1920 video.
  3. Review. The video arrives in the owner's Telegram bot, where it is checked before it goes out.
  4. Post. With one tap in the bot the video is sent to the connected TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Connect a TikTok account

The account owner signs in with TikTok and allows Feedlyx to read the account's basic profile (name and avatar, shown in the bot) and to upload videos to it. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings.
